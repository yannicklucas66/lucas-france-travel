Process

How it works

Four steps, from a first conversation to a finished itinerary you can act on immediately.

01

Discovery call

A free 20-minute call to understand your pace, budget, interests, and what a great trip looks like for you specifically.

02

A short questionnaire

Dates, must-sees, things to avoid, dietary needs, mobility — the details that make an itinerary actually fit your trip.

03

Your itinerary, delivered

A detailed day-by-day plan: regions and towns, lodging options (not reservations), how to move between places, and the kind of local tips you'd only get from someone who grew up there.

04

You book, I stay on call

You reserve everything yourself, directly with the provider, at the best available rate. I'm reachable by message before and during your trip if plans need to shift.
